Bookie

Automated bookkeeping for Husband.LLC.

Bookie is a private bookkeeping assistant that connects to QuickBooks Online to categorize transactions, reconcile accounts, and prepare reports for our accountant. It is operated solely for Husband.LLC and is not offered to the public.
